Good to know

  • Canteens are mostly open around lunch on weekdays. In the evening and at weekends, Hubble is usually your best bet.
  • TU/e doesn't list weekend hours for the canteens, so they are shown as closed at weekends.
  • Hours often change during holidays, the summer and exam weeks (sometimes with extra dinner options).
