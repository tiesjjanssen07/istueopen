Good to know

  • Tap ☆ next to a name to pin it to the top. With Share (in your list) you can send someone a link to that building.
  • Times are Dutch time (Europe/Amsterdam). The page updates itself every 30 seconds.
  • Orange (⚠️) means a building closes within 30 minutes.
  • On a phone, use the buttons at the top to jump to a section, and All / Open now / Study spots to filter the list.
  • Hours can differ during holidays, the Christmas break, the summer and exam periods.
  • Buildings with a reception are only accessible during opening hours. Some buildings need a campus card.
  • See every building on the campus map, with walking routes.
  • Hungry? See where you can get food right now.
  • Tip: add this site to your phone's home screen (browser menu → “Add to Home screen”) to open it like an app.
