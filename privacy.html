Privacy

Short version: this site has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. It does not know who you are. The only personal data it can receive is what you choose to type into the report form.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who runs this site?

Is the TU/e open? is an unofficial, non-commercial project by a TU/e student. It is not made by or affiliated with TU/e. For questions about your data, use the report form (choose “Something else”).

Stored only on your own device

The buildings you pin with ☆ are saved in your browser (local storage). They are never sent anywhere. You can remove them by tapping ⭐ again or by clearing your browser data.

Links to other sites

Building names open Google Maps, and some links go to TU/e websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Nothing is loaded from them until you click.

Your rights

  • Please don't put personal information in a report. If you did, ask via the report form and it will be deleted.
  • Under the GDPR (AVG) you can ask what data about you is stored, and have it corrected or deleted.
  • If you think something is wrong, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
